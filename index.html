01

VPN traffic and Android VpnService

xCH VPN uses Android’s VpnService API to create a device-level encrypted tunnel interface and forward network traffic through proxy or VPN servers that you manually add or import (including VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, TUIC, WireGuard, Snell, AnyTLS, NaiveProxy, SOCKS5, and HTTP/HTTPS).

BUCHU does not operate VPN exit servers and does not log, inspect, intercept, sell, or share your network traffic. We never collect your browsing history, destination websites or IP addresses, DNS queries, connection timestamps, or tunnel payload contents.

When you connect, your encrypted traffic travels directly between your device and the server you configured. Your server operator’s own privacy policy applies to traffic handled by their server. The tunnel service runs in an isolated background process with no analytics, advertising, or account SDKs loaded inside it.

02

Data stored locally on your device

Your configurations and preferences are stored strictly in private app-internal storage on your device. xCH VPN disables Android’s automatic cloud backup (allowBackup="false") so private credentials and configurations are not uploaded to external backup services.

Local dataHow it is used
Server profiles & .xch configsSaved locally in private app storage so you can select, manage, and connect to your servers. Never uploaded to BUCHU.
Subscriptions & routing rulesSubscription URLs, address masks, DNS resolvers, and per-app proxy choices are saved locally. Subscription refreshes connect only to URLs you add.
App preferencesSettings such as Normal/Gaming mode, MTU, IPv6, speed limits, and auto-connect preferences remain on your device.
Local installation IDA random UUID generated on-device and shown only if you explicitly choose Copy on the About screen for support reference.
Session & engine logsHeld in a small bounded memory buffer on-device for the Logs tab. Not uploaded remotely; cleared when you tap Clear or close the session.
Announcements cacheCached read-only team notices and read markers stored locally so unread counts stay accurate offline.
03

Optional Google Sign-In

You can use xCH VPN completely in Guest mode without creating an account or signing in.

If you voluntarily choose Sign in with Google from the account sheet, xCH VPN uses Android’s Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication (provided by Google) to verify your sign-in. When signed in, the app displays your Google profile display name and email address on your device and stores an authentication session managed by Firebase Auth.

BUCHU does not sell your email address or account details, does not link your VPN traffic or server configurations to your Google account, and lets you sign out at any time from the account sheet.

04

Diagnostics, analytics, and announcements

xCH VPN contains no advertising SDKs and does not share data with ad networks or data brokers. To maintain stability and deliver developer notices, the main UI process uses the following Google Firebase services:

ServiceData processed & privacy safeguards
Firebase Crashlytics & NDKCollects app and native crash stack traces, app version, Android API level, device architecture, and scrubbed state breadcrumbs (screen name, protocol type, VPN state). Proxy links and URLs are automatically redacted before logging.
Firebase AnalyticsCollects privacy-safe aggregate telemetry such as screen views, connection attempt/success/failure categories, active protocol slug (for example, vless or wireguard), Gaming mode flag, and coarse server-count buckets. Never logs server addresses, IP addresses, credentials, proxy URIs, search queries, or user traffic.
Developer Announcements (Firestore REST)Performs read-only HTTPS requests to fetch public xCH team announcements and maintenance notices. Sends no user profiles, server configs, or personal information.

Google’s processing of Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, and Firestore traffic is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Android permissions

xCH VPN requests only permissions needed for VPN operation and optional user-initiated features:

PermissionPurpose
BIND_VPN_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEEstablish and keep the user-initiated VPN tunnel and Quick Settings tile running reliably in the foreground.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGESDisplay installed apps in the Per-App Proxy screen so you can include or exclude specific apps from the VPN tunnel. Your installed app list is processed strictly on-device and is never uploaded, logged, or shared.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_NETWORK_STATEConnect to your chosen VPN server, detect Wi-Fi/mobile network transitions for seamless reconnect, run endpoint reachability probes you initiate, refresh user-added subscriptions, and fetch announcements.
CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGEOptional. Used only when you choose to scan a server QR code with your camera or pick an image/file to import a configuration.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATEOptional. Display the persistent VPN connection notification and optional live speed indicator.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDOptional. Reconnect automatically after device restart only if you enable Start on boot in settings.
06

Data security and .xch files

We apply strong security practices to protect your local configurations and app connections. Automatic Android cloud backups and insecure cleartext HTTP connections are disabled by default, and your server profiles stay in private on-device storage.

Exported .xch configuration files are encrypted and protected on your device, with optional password protection when sharing configurations.

07

Data retention, control, and account deletion

Local data: You can delete individual servers, subscriptions, or routing rules inside the app, clear session logs from the Logs tab, or erase all local app data at any time via Android’s Settings → Apps → xCH VPN → Storage → Clear storage or by uninstalling the app.

Optional Google Sign-In account deletion: If you signed in with Google and wish to have your Firebase Authentication account record permanently deleted, sign out in the app and email su.dev.x51@gmail.com from your signed-in email address with the subject “xCH VPN Account Deletion Request”. We will delete the authentication record and confirm completion within 30 days. Crashlytics and Analytics diagnostic records are automatically retained and expired according to Firebase’s standard retention windows (typically 90 days for Crashlytics).

Because xCH VPN never uploads your server configs, routing rules, or VPN traffic to BUCHU, uninstalling the app or clearing app storage immediately removes all local configuration data.
